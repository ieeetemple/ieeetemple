About Us

Powering the Future

IEEE Power & Energy Society (PES) is the premier global provider of scientific and engineering information on electric power and energy, with approximately 40,000 members worldwide. PES sets the standard for engagement, education, and development in the power and energy industry.

At Temple University, our PES chapter focuses on electric power systems, smart grid technologies, renewable energy, and the transition to sustainable power infrastructure. We explore everything from power generation and transmission to energy markets and policy.

Focus Areas

What We Explore

Power Generation & Transmission

Electric power generation, high-voltage transmission, grid infrastructure, and power system analysis and protection.

Smart Grid & Electrification

Smart grid technologies, grid modernization, demand response, microgrids, and the future of electrification.

Sustainable Energy

Renewable energy integration, energy storage, wind and solar power, and the transition to clean energy systems.

Our Activities

What We Do

Standards Development

Participate in developing IEEE standards that shape the global power and energy industry. Nearly half of all IEEE standards come from PES.

Education & Webinars

Technical workshops, "Plain Talk" webinars, tutorials on power systems, energy markets, and policy regulation.

Conferences & Mentorship

Attend PES General Meeting, Grid Edge, and T&D conferences. Connect with industry mentors through our workforce initiative.
