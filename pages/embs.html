About Us

Where Engineering Meets Healthcare

IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society (EMBS) is one of the largest technical societies within IEEE, with over 13,000 members in 97+ countries. EMBS advances the application of engineering sciences and technology to medicine and biology, promoting the profession and providing global leadership for the benefit of its members and humanity.

At Temple University, our EMBS chapter brings together students interested in biomedical engineering, healthcare technology, and the intersection of engineering with life sciences. We explore diagnostic systems, therapeutic technologies, bioinformatics, robotics, and AI in healthcare.
