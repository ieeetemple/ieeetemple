About Us

Empowering Women in Tech

IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) is one of the largest international professional organizations dedicated to promoting women engineers and scientists, inspiring girls around the world to follow their academic interests in a career in engineering and science.

At Temple University, our WIE affinity group works alongside the IEEE student branch to create an inclusive community, provide mentorship opportunities, and host events that support the professional development of women in ECE.
