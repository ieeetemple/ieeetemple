About BizTech

Where Ideas Meet Execution

At BizTech, we believe innovation happens at the intersection of business and technology. Our mission is to bring together business-minded leaders and tech-driven problem solvers to work on projects that create real-world impact.

Whether you're a business student interested in product innovation, or an engineering student curious about entrepreneurship, BizTech is the place to connect, collaborate, and grow.

Why Join?

Benefits for Everyone

For Business Students

  • Learn how technology shapes industries
  • Collaborate on technical projects
  • Gain hands-on innovation experience
  • Understand product development cycles

For Engineering Students

  • Develop entrepreneurial thinking
  • Learn how to pitch ideas effectively
  • Understand the business side of tech
  • Build market-ready solutions

For Everyone

  • Build valuable professional networks
  • Gain interdisciplinary teamwork experience
  • Bring your ideas to life
  • Prepare for real-world careers
